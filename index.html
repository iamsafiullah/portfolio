    How do I get my business found on Google and ChatGPT?

    I optimize your website so it shows up in Google search and in AI answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews. That means clear content, fast pages, correct business details, and trusted information — so customers can find and call you.

    How do I get found by voice search and quick answers?

    I structure your content so search engines can pick it as a direct answer — for featured snippets, "People Also Ask" boxes, and voice assistants like Siri and Alexa. Short, clear answers to real customer questions.

    SEO services in Abu Dhabi typically range from AED 1,500 to AED 10,000+ per month depending on scope, competition, and goals. Safi Ullah offers flexible freelance SEO consulting tailored to UAE businesses of all sizes.

    SEO typically takes 3-6 months to show meaningful results. Low-competition long-tail keywords can rank within weeks, while competitive keywords may take 6-12 months. Technical SEO fixes can improve rankings faster.
