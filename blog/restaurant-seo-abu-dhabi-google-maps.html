Restaurant SEO in Abu Dhabi: How to Get More Customers from Google Maps and Search

By Safi Ullah, SEO specialist in Abu Dhabi. Updated 2026-11-27.

Short answer: most people choose a restaurant from Google Maps or a quick search. To be picked, your Google Business Profile must be complete, your website must show your menu as text, and your reviews must be real and answered.

1. Start with your Google Business Profile

This is your most important page. Claim and verify it, then fill in every field.

  • Category: choose the one main category that fits (for example a restaurant type or cafe), then add real secondary ones.
  • Name, address, phone: use exactly what is on your signage and your website. Include the area (for example Khalidiyah, Al Reem Island or Mussafah) in your address, not in the business name.
  • Hours: keep them current, including Ramadan, public holidays and special days.
  • Menu and links: add your menu, and link to your booking or WhatsApp ordering page.
  • Photos: real photos of dishes, the inside, the outside and the entrance. Add new ones regularly.

More detail in the Google Business Profile checklist for Abu Dhabi.

2. Put your menu on your website as text

A menu that is only a PDF or a photo is hard for Google and AI tools to read. Add a normal web page with dish names, short descriptions and prices. Keep it in sync with the menu in the restaurant. If you serve halal, vegetarian or vegan options, say so in plain words on the page.

3. Write for the searches people actually make

Think about how a hungry customer searches, then make sure your pages answer it clearly:

  • The type of food plus the area, for example "Lebanese restaurant Khalidiyah".
  • The situation, for example "family restaurant with a kids area" or "breakfast cafe near Corniche", if that is true for you.
  • Practical questions: parking, reservations, delivery, opening times, prayer-friendly times in Ramadan.

Use one page per branch. If you have several locations, give each its own page with its own address, phone number and map.

4. Add English and Arabic where your customers use both

Abu Dhabi customers search in both languages. If you add Arabic, have a native speaker write or check it. Do not use unedited machine translation for your menu or address.

5. Reviews: ask honestly and reply to all

  • Ask real guests after a good meal. A QR code on the table or a WhatsApp message with your review link works well.
  • Reply to every review. Thank people. If it is negative, stay calm, say what you will do and offer to talk offline.
  • Never buy reviews, write your own, or offer free food for a five-star rating. This breaks Google's rules and can get your listing removed.

6. Make the website fast and easy on a phone

Most restaurant searches happen on a phone. Check that your phone number, directions and menu are one tap away, and that pages load quickly on mobile data. Test your own site on your own phone.

7. Help AI search understand you

Answer the common questions on a short FAQ on your site: opening hours, booking, delivery, dietary options, parking. Keep your details the same everywhere. AI tools and search engines both rely on clear, consistent information. No one can guarantee that a business will appear in an AI answer, so focus on being clear and trustworthy.

8. Track what matters

In Google Business Profile, watch calls, direction requests and website clicks. In Google Analytics and Search Console, watch visits to your menu and booking pages. Review once a month and fix what is weak.

Where to start this week

  1. Verify and complete your Google Business Profile.
  2. Upload 10 real, recent photos.
  3. Put your menu on your website as text.
  4. Send your review link to your next ten happy guests.

Frequently asked questions

How can a restaurant in Abu Dhabi show up on Google Maps?

Claim and verify your Google Business Profile, choose accurate categories, keep your name, address, phone and hours exact, add real photos and collect honest reviews.

Should my menu be a PDF?

Put it on a normal web page as text. A PDF or photo alone is harder for search engines and AI tools to read.

Can SEO guarantee I will be number one?

No. Nobody can guarantee a ranking. Good SEO improves your chances by making your business clear, complete and trustworthy.

Do I need an Arabic version of my site?

If many of your customers search in Arabic, yes. Have a native speaker write or check it.
