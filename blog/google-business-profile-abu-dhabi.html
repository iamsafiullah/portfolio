Google Business Profile for Abu Dhabi Businesses: Local SEO Checklist

By Safi Ullah, SEO specialist in Abu Dhabi. Updated 2026-11-27.

Short answer: claim and verify your profile, choose the right categories, keep your details exact, add real photos, and ask happy customers for honest reviews.

1. Claim and verify the profile

Search for your business name on Google. If a profile exists, claim it. If not, create one at business.google.com and complete Google's verification. Only a verified profile can be managed fully.

2. Choose accurate categories

Pick the one primary category that best describes your main service, then add secondary ones that are truly offered. Do not add categories for services you do not provide.

3. Keep name, address and phone exact

Use your real business name as it appears on your signage and licence. Use the same address format and phone number on your website, Google profile and directories. Abu Dhabi addresses can be written several ways. Pick one and stay consistent.

4. Fill in everything

  • Opening hours, including holiday changes.
  • Service list and a short, honest description.
  • Website link to the most relevant page.
  • Real photos of your place, team and work.

5. Ask for reviews the right way

Ask real customers after a good job. Share your review link by WhatsApp. Reply to every review, good or bad. Never buy reviews or offer rewards for positive ones. This breaks Google's rules.

6. Link it to your website

Make sure your website has a page for each service and a contact page with the same details as your profile. See my SEO services in Abu Dhabi.
