Technical SEO Audit in the UAE

A clear list of what is stopping your site from ranking, in order of what to fix first.

What I check

  • Crawling and indexing: robots.txt, sitemap, canonical tags, redirects, duplicate pages, noindex mistakes.
  • Speed and Core Web Vitals: LCP, INP and CLS on mobile and desktop.
  • Mobile usability: layout, tap targets, text size.
  • Structured data: valid schema that matches what is on the page.
  • On-page basics: titles, descriptions, headings, image alt text, internal links.
  • AI crawler access: whether search and AI crawlers are allowed to read your content.

What you get

A written report with each issue, why it matters and how to fix it. I can also implement the fixes. The audit is part of the Basic SEO package from AED 1,500 per month.
